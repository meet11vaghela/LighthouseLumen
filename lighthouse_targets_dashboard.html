Target tracking rules

  1. Sales Value = Sales Units Target × Approximate Ticket Size.
  2. Revenue Target is calculated at 2% of sales value, as specified in MVT.xlsx.
  3. Lead channel mix is standardized to WhatsApp 10%, ConvoAI 30%, and Digital 60% of qualified leads. Digital is split 60% Meta and 40% Website.
  4. Walk-in reference split and qualified-lead requirements are retained from MVT.xlsx. Rounded channel targets may vary by one lead due to rounding.
  5. Use this as the target baseline; enter actuals monthly in a separate performance tracker to measure achievement.
